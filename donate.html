FRC Team 7250, proudly representing Oscoda, Michigan, is a passionate group of high school students united by a love for innovation, teamwork, and robotics. In 2026, our team had an incredible year, pushing the limits of creativity and determination at every level of the FIRST Robotics Competition.
From the beginning of the season, we faced tough competition in our district events, but our commitment and collaboration propelled us forward. Each challenge became an opportunity to learn and refine our robot, ultimately earning us a spot at the state championships. There, we played as an alliance captain, guiding our team with leadership and strategic thinking to secure a place at the World competition in Houston.
At worlds, where we were surrounded by teams from across the globe, we showcased not only our robot’s capabilities but also the spirit and capabilities of our community. Every match was a testament to countless hours of hard work, late nights, and the unwavering support from mentors, families, and local partners.
Our journey is about more than just building robots, rather, it’s about inspiring future leaders, fostering STEM education, and making a positive impact in our community. With every challenge we overcome, we’re reminded of what’s possible when passionate minds come together to achieve a common goal.
